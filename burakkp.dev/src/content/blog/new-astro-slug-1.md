## From an idea to a useful note

This notebook has room for Go, Cloudflare Workers, Linux administration, and OpenSCAD. The common thread is understanding how things work by building them.

A useful engineering note can be small. Start with a question, show a minimal example, and record what you learned.

## A format worth keeping

1. Describe the problem and the environment.
2. Include enough context to reproduce the result.
3. Explain the decision, including its trade-offs.
4. Finish with a way to verify the outcome.

Code, diagrams, and commands should support the explanation. They do not have to carry the whole story.
